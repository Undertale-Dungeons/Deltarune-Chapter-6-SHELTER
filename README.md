# DELTARUNE: SHELTER

*A fan-made, non-canon DELTARUNE Chapter 6 concept.*

DELTARUNE: SHELTER is a story-driven RPG fan project built around a mysterious trip to the bunker at the edge of Hometown. Kris and Susie enter a place that seems to stretch far beyond its walls, where reflections, strange messages, and familiar faces may not be what they seem.

> **Status:** Early project setup. Story, encounters, and mechanics described here are design goals and may change as development continues. This repository is not affiliated with or endorsed by Toby Fox.

## Story premise

Kris and Susie make their way to the bunker at the bottom of Hometown. After forcing their way inside, the scene gives way to darkness. Kris later finds themself in a strange mirror room filled with seemingly endless reflections and a book marked **ENTRY 1**.

As the journey continues, the pair encounter unsettling creatures, unexpected allies, and a threat connected to the Knight.

## Planned features

- A new story chapter set around the Hometown bunker
- Original rooms, puzzles, dialogue, and encounters
- Turn-based battles with custom ACT options
- A **PATIENCE SOUL** parry mechanic
- Original enemy concepts, including **HELP ME** and **FRIENDSPAWN**
- Major boss encounters, including **YOU** and a later fusion-inspired boss
- Multiple battle outcomes that can affect later scenes

All features are planned concepts until they are implemented in the game.

## Gameplay notes

Battles are intended to reward paying attention and choosing ACT options thoughtfully. Some opponents may punish rushing straight into FIGHT. Learn an enemy's behavior, experiment with ACT, and watch for changes in its dialogue.

The PATIENCE SOUL mechanic is planned around timing and defensive play. Exact controls and timings will be documented once they are implemented.

## Hints

These hints are intentionally vague so they do not spoil the story.

1. **The bunker:** Pay attention to what Kris and Susie bring with them. The way they enter matters.
2. **The mirror room:** Do not assume every reflection is just decoration.
3. **The book:** If you find **ENTRY 1**, take time to read it carefully.
4. **Battles:** ACT is not just flavor text. It can be important to understanding an enemy.
5. **YOU:** If an attack seems unusually ineffective, consider whether the fight is asking you to do something else first.
6. **PATIENCE:** Defense is an active part of the encounter. Watch the pattern instead of rushing.
